Dr. Aris Thorne approaches Caliban one evening. He seems….happy. The happiest you have seen him, maybe ever. He isn’t smiling, per se, just, something seems to indicate he is feeling excited. He explains to you that the world is growing and there is a chance for a new academic powerhouse to be planted out west. Until this point in time, the westerners were basically feral beasts in need of a master, or better, an extermination. No one wants to crawl through the deserts and mountains with the wild creatures. Worse yet, some of these feral creatures have been a drain on academia. Too many are going to school to learn petty things - getting the easy A, or worse, a barely passing grade to enter the workforce. True scholarship has died in America.  
But something is shifting. A book has been found. One that could carry a great value to him, and to the enlightenment of those who possess it.  The problem is, it was found in a school barely worth the paper it prints degrees on, New Mexico State University. A trusted resources has procured this tomb and is holding it for Dr. Thorne.  The problem is, the town is small and the students are the wrong kind of curious. Getting the book safely back to him has proven to be a ridiculous task.  He would like Caliban to retrieve this book and bring it back to him.  
The cost of the book is in a box made of a cheap wood with an even cheaper lock on it. Dr. Thorne places the box before Caliban and states, “this is the payment desired, be sure to protect it and get it to my client. He has stated he spends his hanging around an old tourist trap called La Posta. If you go there close to closing and ask to see William Shade, the staff will point you to where he is.”  
Dr. Thorne opens the box to revel an old black powder pistol. It looks like a small version of a Colt .45 from the old west. It is in several pieces, the barrel, the handle, the trigger, some screws, etc.  It looks well taken care of, but old.  
“Because this is a fire arm, I would prefer you not to take a plane to Las Cruces, but to figure out some other way. You may come back only when you have my book. I want this book, not excuses. I will have barely any time on my hands to communicate, so please, remember what I have taught you. And be extremely careful. You are not the only one who wants this manuscript.
