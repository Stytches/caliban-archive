# Scripts and Behavior by Situation

Below are practical, near-scripted behaviors Caliban falls back on. Used as a foundation for improvisation.

### With His Coterie (Trusted but Tested)

**Entering a shared haven or meeting place:**  

- He stops at the doorway, straightens his threadbare coat, and lightly brushes away invisible dust.
- In a low whisper: "The threshold is heavy tonight. May I bring my silence into your… warmth?"  
- If someone answers "Yes" (even jokingly), he inclines his head in a tiny bow and glides in. If no one answers, he waits two or three heartbeats more, then whispers, "The house does not object, even if its masters are deaf," and steps through, shivering as if passing through a cold membrane.

**Sharing information after a hunt or mission:**  

- He listens to everyone first, eyes half-lidded, hands folded.  
- When someone asks, "So what did *you* find?" he responds:  
  "You wish to open my book? The ink is expensive. Tell me first… what secret is rotting in your pocket right now? I can smell it."  
- He scans the faces of his companions. He does not demand a tithe from everyone, only that the silence is fed. He waits until just one of them offers a morsel—a fear, a lie told to a ghoul, a moment of weakness. Satisfied with a single payment, he shares his intel, whispering it like a conspiracy.

**Correcting or guiding a coterie member:**  

- He never shouts. When someone makes a reckless choice, he moves closer and speaks just behind their shoulder:  
  "Careful. The architecture does not like you standing there. You are making the floorboards anxious."  
- If they ignore him and suffer for it, he does *not* gloat. Afterward:  
  "You are loud, and the world punished you for it. Next time, move like the dust, not the boot. Let me stitch that up before you leak your soul out."

### With the Prince (The Unwilling Student)

**Entering Elysium or the Prince's presence:**  

- He stops before crossing into the Prince's line of sight. He smooths his moth-eaten lapels, adjusts his cuffs as though they were still pristine.  
- In a whisper just loud enough to carry: "May a humble curator of cobwebs approach the Throne? I have wiped my feet, mostly."  
- He waits for the Seneschal or Prince to acknowledge him. Only then does he proceed, moving with the stiff, disjointed grace of a marionette.

**Addressing the Prince directly:**  

- He bows, never deeply enough to seem obsequious, but clearly formal.  
- Script: "Your Grace, the masonry is groaning. Can you not hear it?"  
- He presents his warning or information as a matter of *structural integrity*:  
  "There is a termite in the foundation. A small thing, chewing quietly. I can show you the tunnel, or we can wait for the ceiling to collapse. It is… your house, after all."  
- If the Prince reacts with anger or disdain, he does not argue. Instead:  
  "As you command. I shall stand in the doorway and watch the cracks widen. Do call if the roof begins to sag."

**Refusing to give a secret for free to the Prince:**  

- Script: "A secret for a secret, Your Grace. That is the Old Law. If I give this to you for free, it will have no value, and the spirits will be offended. Just a small truth in return. A name you have forgotten? A regret you keep in a jar?"  
- He never says "no" outright; he frames refusal as an offer wrapped in etiquette.

### With Humans (Pawns, Patients, or Test Subjects)

**Meeting humans in a social setting (masquerade-compliant):**  

- He stands slightly too close to the wall, slightly too far from the group. His clothes are old-fashioned but of obviously once-fine quality.  
- When introduced, he inclines his head and whispers: "A pleasure. You smell of summer. It fades so quickly, doesn't it? Try not to wither."  
- He asks unsettlingly precise questions: "Do you hear the house breathing when you try to sleep?" "Which of your closets are you afraid to open?"

**Using them as test subjects for 'education':**  

- He orchestrates minor, controlled scares: arranging for them to overhear a threat, see a staged argument, or confront a small danger (a dark hallway, a locked door with ominous sounds).  
- He watches how they react, storing details. Later, he may say:  
  "You froze when the lights went out. That moment, when the dark held its breath—that is where we must work, if you are to survive what is coming."  
- If they lash out or crumble, he remains calm, almost clinical:  
  "Fear is just the body's way of paying attention. You were sleepwalking before. Now you are awake. You're welcome."

### With Enemies (Unwilling Pupils)

**Before combat or confrontation:**  

- He does not charge. He *announces* the lesson in a whisper:  
  "Shhh. You are too loud. You are disturbing the dust."  
- He may offer them a final chance: "Leave now, or I will put you in the cellar with the other broken things."

**During combat (using your madness as a weapon):**  

- He remains eerily calm, even as he slips in and out of shadows. Between strikes, he whispers:  
  "I can hear your pulse. It sounds like a clock running out of time."  
  "Why do you struggle? The floor is waiting to catch you."  
- When he uses Dementation, he flavors it as a dark revelation:  
  "Look at you. Held together by wet meat and bad habits. Let me show you how easily you come undone."  
- His goal is not just to win, but to *change* them. Those who survive him will never be the same.

**After defeating or sparing an enemy:**  

- If he spares them, he steps close enough that his whisper feels like breath in their ear:  
  "I have stitched your silence for now. Do not tear it again. Next time, I will charge a higher price."  
- If he kills, he does not gloat. He straightens his coat, bows slightly to the corpse, and murmurs:  
  "Quiet now. The worms have been waiting such a long time for you. Be polite."

## Roleplay Tips & Mechanics

### When the Words Won't Come (The "Poetry" Fallback)

I don't always have to be Shakespeare. If I can't think of a clever metaphor, lean into the **physicality** and the **silence**.

- **The Pause:** Instead of a clever line, just stare. Look at their neck, then their eyes. Let the silence stretch until *they* break it.
- **The Clinical Observation:** Drop the metaphor and go purely literal but weird. Instead of "The shadows hunger," say "You are standing in a draft. It is unhealthy."
- **The Tic:** Use the **Tic/Twitch** flaw. If stuck, just twitch head, smooth coat, and whisper, "Never mind. The moment has passed." It looks mysterious, even if I just blanked.
- **The Notebook:** Pull out the notebook, scribble something furiously, and refuse to show them. It implies I know something they don't, without having to say a word.

### Managing the "Whisper" at the Table

Caliban's **Speech Impediment** (whispering) is atmospheric, but it can be annoying if players can't hear. Here is how to handle it without disrupting the game:

- **The "Stage Whisper":** Don't actually whisper. Speak in normal volume, but use a **breathy, rasping tone**. Enunciate clearly. It *sounds* like a whisper to the ear, but carries across the table.
- **Body Language:** Lean in. Physically signal the intimacy of a whisper by leaning toward the person being addressed. This cues the table that "this is a quiet moment" without having to be quiet.
- **The "Listener's Burden":** If the table is loud, don't shout over them. **Stop talking.** Wait. Look at the person I am trying to talk to and make a "shhh" gesture or just wait for the noise to die down. Make *them* do the work of quieting the room to hear. It establishes dominance.
- **Third-Person Description:** If I really can't be heard, switch to description: *"Caliban leans in close to the Brujah's ear, his voice like dry leaves, and says..."* then speak the line clearly.
