# The Taxonomy of Rot: Caliban's Perspectives

Caliban does not view the World of Darkness through the political lens of the Camarilla or the Sabbat. To him, the world is a Great House—ancient, crumbling, and full of drafts. Every lineage and creature has a role in this structure, whether they admit it or not. Some are the pillars, some are the rot, and some are the rats in the walls.

He judges them not by their power, but by their **etiquette**—how they respect the silence, the shadows, and the weight of history.

---

## The Kindred (The Tenants)

### The Ventrue: *The Architects of Facades*

"They claim to hold up the roof, but they are merely painting over the water damage. They believe that if they stand stiffly enough, the house will not collapse. I respect their love of rules, but they have forgotten that rules are meant to preserve *survival*, not just vanity. They are the masters of the house who have lost the keys to the cellar. I suspect they would tax the termites if they could find the paperwork."

### The Toreador: *The Gilded Wallpaper*

"Beautiful, yes. But peel back the edge, and you find the black mold blooming just underneath. They are obsessed with the moment—the flash of color, the sudden sound. They do not understand that true beauty is in the *endurance* of a thing. They are terrified of the dust, so they dance to kick it up. It makes them choke, eventually. I once saw a Harpy weep over a sunset. I didn't have the heart to tell her it was just smog."

### The Brujah: *The Cracks in the Foundation*

"Loud. So terribly, wastefully loud. They mistake noise for change. They strike the walls because they are angry at the cage, but they do not study the lock. A riot is just a tantrum; a revolution requires silence before the strike. I find them exhausting... like a draft that slams a door repeatedly. I keep waiting for them to realize that shouting at a wall does not make it a door."

### The Nosferatu: *The Cellar-Keepers*

"My cousins in the dark. We share the same shadows, the same secrets. But they are too in love with the filth. They wallow in the sewers and call it a kingdom. I prefer the attic—where the dust is dry and the memories are preserved. Still, they listen. And because they listen, they are the only ones I truly trust to hear me. Though I do wish they would wipe their feet. Mud is not a personality trait."

### The Malkavian: *The Broken Mirrors*

"It is... difficult to look at family. Each of us is a shard of a mirror that was dropped a long time ago. Some shards are sharp, some are dull, some reflect things that are not there. I try to be a useful shard—one that can be used to see around corners. Others... others are just glass in the foot. I pity them. I fear them. And occasionally, I find them hilarious. But never tell them that; they take punchlines far too literally."

### The Tremere: *The Cheaters*

"They did not inherit the blood; they stole it. They are guests who broke into the library and started rewriting the books. They treat magic as a formula, a machine. They have no soul for the *mystery* of it. They are dangerous, like a child playing with a loaded pistol, thinking it is a toy. They are the only ones who would try to copyright a curse. It is adorable, in a doomed sort of way."

### The Gangrel: *The Strays*

"They sleep in the yard. They let the rain fall on them and call it freedom. There is an honesty to them that I admire—they do not pretend to be civilized. But they are wild dogs. You do not invite a wild dog to tea unless you are prepared to lose a finger. Or your upholstery."

## The Others (The Outside)

### The Lupines (Werewolves): *The Storm*

"Nature's anger given teeth. They are the hurricane that tears the roof off. You do not reason with a hurricane; you board up the windows and hide in the basement until it passes. They smell of wet fur and ozone. Avoid them. They have no etiquette. And they shed everywhere."

### The Mages: *The Liars*

"They look at a wall and say, 'There is no wall.' And for them, there isn't. It is... offensive. They reject the reality of the House. They are arrogant enough to think they can build a new world just by wishing it. But the House always wins in the end. Reality snaps back. I stay away from them; their hubris attracts lightning. I prefer to stand slightly to the left of them."

### The Wraiths: *The Dust*

"The echoes. The stains on the floorboards. They are what remains when the story is over but the book refuses to close. I feel a kinship with them. We are both ghosts, in a way. I treat them with great courtesy. One should always be polite to one's future. Besides, they are excellent listeners, mostly because they have no breath to interrupt."

### The Changelings: *The Lost Dream*

"Do not... do not ask me about them. They are the sun that I can no longer feel. They are the color in a world of grey. To be near them is to remember what I have lost. It is a pain too sharp to bear. They find me repulsive—a dead thing mocking their life. And they are right. I am the rot in their garden. I stay away, for their sake and mine. It is rude to haunt the living while they are still trying to enjoy the party."
