# Equipment: The Main-Gauche (Parrying Dagger)

**Description:**
A heavy, wide-bladed dagger with a large basket hilt designed to protect the hand. Caliban keeps this hidden inside his coat, drawing it only when the "Geometry" of the fight requires a "Scalpel." It is used primarily to catch enemy blades and deliver the killing stroke.

**System (V20):**

* **Damage:** Strength + 1 (Lethal)
* **Difficulty:** 6
* **Concealability:** Jacket (Difficulty 7 to spot under a coat).
* **Special:** When used defensively to parry, the difficulty is reduced by 1 (Difficulty 5).
