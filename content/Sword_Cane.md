# Equipment: The Gentleman's Sword Cane

**Description:**
A seemingly ordinary walking stick made of polished ebony with a silver handle. A twist of the handle releases the catch, allowing the user to draw a slender, lethal rapier blade. It is the perfect weapon for Caliban's "Old Money" persona—elegant, unassuming, and deadly.

**System (V20):**

* **Damage:** Strength + 2 (Lethal)
* **Difficulty:** 6
* **Concealability:** N/A (Appears as a normal cane). Detecting the catch requires a Perception + Investigation roll (Difficulty 8).
* **Notes:** When sheathed, it functions as a blunt club (Strength + 1 Bashing damage).
