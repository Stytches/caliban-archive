# Haven: The Carriage House

**Location:** The intact brick carriage house behind the burned-out shell of the Van Der Hoven estate (a Victorian mansion in the Old District).

**Appearance:**
To the outside world, it is an overgrown ruin. The main house is a blackened skeleton, condemned since the 1970s. The carriage house in the rear, however, remains structurally sound, hidden behind a high brick wall choked with ivy that seems to wither and blacken in specific patches (a side effect of Caliban's **Touch of Frost**).

**The Interior:**

* **The Receiving Room:** The only "clean" room. It is set up like a Victorian parlor—faded velvet drapes, a single high-backed wing chair, and a small table with an unlit oil lamp. This is where he conducts "lessons."
* **The Sleeping Quarters:** He does not sleep in a bed. In the loft (formerly for hay), there is a large, ornate steamer trunk lined with grave soil.
* **The Collection:** The rest of the space is filled with "dust"—stacks of yellowed newspapers, broken clocks that all stopped at different times, and taxidermy animals that he sometimes talks to.

**Security:**

* **Seclusion:** The property is rumored to be haunted (which is technically true, given Caliban's presence). Local kids avoid it.
* **The Garden:** The garden between the main house and the carriage house has gone feral. Caliban uses his **Stealth** and **Obfuscate** to move through it without leaving a trace.
* **Locks:** The doors are secured with heavy, antique iron locks that Caliban maintains meticulously.

**Atmosphere:**
It smells of dry rot, old paper, and cold. It is utterly silent. Caliban treats it not as a home, but as a museum of a life he never actually lived.
