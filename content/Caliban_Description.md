# Physical Description: The Ghost in Tweed

**"He looks like something that was buried in a very expensive suit."**

Caliban is a striking, unsettling figure who embodies the concept of "decayed elegance." He does not look like a monster in the visceral sense (like a Nosferatu), but rather like a human who has been left on a shelf for too long—faded, dusty, and dry.

## The Frame

* **Height:** 6'2" (188 cm)
* **Build:** Skeletal. He weighs only 145 lbs, giving him a stretched, elongated appearance. His limbs seem slightly too long for his torso, reinforcing the "marionette" quality of his movement.
* **Posture:** He stands with rigid, perfect verticality, like a soldier or a butler waiting for orders. When he moves, it is with a stiff, disjointed grace—smooth, but mechanical.

## The Face

* **Complexion:** His skin is not just pale; it is *grey*, like old parchment or ash. It pulls tight against his cheekbones and jaw.
* **Eyes:** Deep-set and shadowed. The irises are a washed-out, watery blue, often described as "cloudy glass." He rarely blinks.
* **Hair:** Dark, thinning, and slicked back severely with an oil that smells faintly of formaldehyde and lavender.
* **Expression:** His resting face is a mask of polite, neutral attentiveness. However, his **Tic/Twitch** flaw manifests as a subtle, rhythmic twitch of his left eye or a sudden, bird-like cocking of the head.

## The Wardrobe

Caliban wears the same outfit almost every night, maintained with obsessive care despite its obvious age. It is the uniform of the "gentleman scholar" he tried to be in life, now rotting alongside him.

* **The Coat:** An ankle-length, charcoal wool greatcoat. It is heavy, structured, and clearly expensive, but the hem is moth-eaten and the elbows are worn shiny.
* **The Suit:** Beneath the coat, he wears a three-piece tweed suit in a heather-grey herringbone pattern. It smells of dry rot and cedar.
* **Accessories:**
  * **Gloves:** Grey silk gloves, always worn to hide his cold, dead hands (and to mitigate his **[[Touch of Frost]]**).
  * **The Cane:** A silver-topped ebony cane (concealing his rapier). He leans on it not for support, but for *composition*—using it to frame his pose.
  * **The Scent:** He does not smell of blood. He smells of **old paper, dry earth, and cold air**.

## The Aura (The [[Sluagh]] Influence)

His supernatural nature bleeds into his physical presence.

* **The Chill ([[Touch of Frost]]):** The air around him is always 10 degrees colder than the room. Frost patterns sometimes form on glass when he stands near it for too long.
* **The Stillness ([[Eerie Presence]]):** He possesses the absolute, unnatural stillness of a corpse. He does not breathe, shift his weight, or blink unless he consciously wills it. This lack of biological "noise" triggers a primal unease in observers, who register him as an inanimate object that has wrongly learned to walk.

## The Masquerade (Blush of Life)

When he spends a blood point to mimic life (the **Blush of Life**), the transformation is startling but imperfect.

* **The Color:** The grey recedes, replaced by a feverish, consumptive flush high on his cheekbones. He doesn't look healthy; he looks like a dying Victorian poet—pale, sweaty, and burning with a final illness.
* **The Breath:** He begins to breathe, but the rhythm is too deliberate. It sounds like a bellows being worked by hand—*hiss-in, hiss-out*. It is the breathing of someone trying to remember how to breathe.
* **The Effect:** Instead of making him seem more human, it often pushes him into the "Uncanny Valley." He looks like a wax figure that has begun to melt. Most mortals find this state *more* disturbing than his usual stillness, as it adds a layer of desperate, performative humanity to his corpse-like frame.
