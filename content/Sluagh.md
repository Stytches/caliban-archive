# The Sluagh (The Underhill Court)

> *"We are the things that go bump in the night. But we bump politely."*

In the setting of **Changeling: The Dreaming**, the **Sluagh** (pronounced *sloo-ah*) are one of the Kith (types of fae). They are the nightmares of the fae world—creatures of decay, silence, and secrets. They are often called the "Underhill Court" or "Bogies."

For Caliban, this is the bloodline that runs dormant in his veins. He is not a true Changeling, but a **Kinain** (a human with fae heritage) who was Embraced before he could awaken. His Malkavian madness has twisted this heritage into a permanent, undead caricature of what he should have been.

## Physiology and Appearance

True Sluagh are often described as:

* **Pale and amorphous:** Their skin is often dead-white or grey. They are boneless or have cartilage instead of bone, allowing them to squeeze through impossibly small cracks (under doors, through keyholes).
* **Decayed elegance:** They favor Victorian or antiquated clothing, often tattered but high-quality. They smell of dust, old libraries, and wet earth.
* **Sensitive:** They are physically weak and recoil from bright lights and loud noises.

## Culture and Behavior

### The Law of Whispers

Sluagh cannot speak above a whisper. It is not just a preference; it is a geas (a magical prohibition). To shout is to cause themselves physical pain or lose their magic. This forces others to lean in close to hear them, creating an uncomfortable intimacy.

### Hoarders of Secrets

They value information more than gold. A Sluagh will trade a bag of diamonds for a juicy, shameful secret about a local Duke. They are the spies, the archivists, and the blackmailers of fae society. They believe that knowing a thing gives you power over it.

### Rigid Etiquette

Despite being "monsters," they are the most polite of all Kith. They adhere to ancient, complex rules of hospitality and address. They will never break a promise, but they will twist the wording to their advantage. They view rudeness as a sign of weakness and chaos.

### The High Court of Dust

They tend to dwell in underground places—sewers, basements, crypts, and abandoned libraries. They find comfort in the dark and the quiet, places where "the silence has weight."

## Caliban as a "Rotten Sluagh"

Caliban represents a **tragic inversion** of the Sluagh archetype.

* **The Whisper:** Like a Sluagh, he cannot shout. But for him, it is a psychological block (a Flaw) rather than a magical geas.
* **The Secrets:** He hoards secrets, but he uses them to "teach" (Malkavian) rather than just to hoard or blackmail.
* **The Etiquette:** His obsession with rules is a derangement, a desperate attempt to mimic the Sluagh culture he feels in his blood but can never truly join.
* **The Tragedy:** To a real Sluagh, Caliban is an abomination. He is a "statue" of a Sluagh—frozen, dead, and lacking the spark of life (Glamour). He is a nightmare that died and forgot to fade away.
