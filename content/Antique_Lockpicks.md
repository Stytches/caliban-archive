# Equipment: Antique Locksmith's Set

**Description:**
A worn leather roll containing a set of tempered steel picks with yellowed ivory handles. They look more like Victorian surgical instruments than modern burglar's tools. While the handles are genuine 19th-century ivory, the business ends have been meticulously replaced with modern spring steel, hand-filed by Caliban to manipulate contemporary pin-tumbler locks without breaking the aesthetic. Caliban treats them with the reverence of a librarian handling rare keys.

**Justification:**
Caliban does not view himself as a thief. He is a "Historian of Closed Spaces." To him, a locked door is simply a book that hasn't been opened yet.

* **The "Yielding" Mechanism:** Crucially, these tools allow him to bypass his **Obsessive Ritualism (Permission to Enter)**. If he defeats the lock, he considers the door to have "yielded" and granted him permission to enter. Without them, he would be trapped outside any locked room unless invited.

**System (V20):**

* **Effect:** Essential for using the **Larceny** skill to pick locks without penalty.
* **Quality:** High quality (Antique but well-maintained). May grant a -1 Difficulty to Larceny rolls on older, mechanical locks (Storyteller discretion).
